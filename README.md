# Privacy Policy for Mizo-Karbi Dictionary

**Effective Date:** October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your personal information is handled in the **Karbi Mizo Dictionary** mobile application.

**Karbi Mizo Dictionary** is designed to work as an offline dictionary tool. 
- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts).
- **No Account Required:** You can use all features of the app without creating an account or registering.

### 2. Device Permissions
The app does not require sensitive device permissions (such as GPS location, camera, or microphone) to function. Standard internet access may be used solely for downloading updates or displaying static content if applicable.

### 3. Third-Party Services
The app does not use third-party services that collect information used to identify you.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13 specifically, but because we do not collect any personal data, it is completely safe for users of all ages, including children.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
